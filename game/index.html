Ember Duel

A five-trick card duel on a riverboat table. Outplay Captain Mira card by card: every trick you win burns her life away — every trick you lose burns yours.

How to play: Click one of your five cards each trick. Power = card value + suit bonus.
▲ Ember scorches ❀ Bloom · ❀ Bloom drinks ● Tide · ● Tide douses ▲ Ember — a winning suit adds +3.
★ Star cards always add +2. Higher power wins; the loser loses life equal to the gap (a tie singes both for 1).
The winner leads the next trick. When Mira leads, her card lies face-up on the table and every card in your hand shows the exact power it would score against it — dump a low card, or spend the one card that beats her.
First duelist to reach 0 life loses the game. New hands are dealt after five tricks.
